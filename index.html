Shadowville

Shadowville is a personal studio app run by one creator. A small crew of AI agents helps write, voice and animate original short suspense stories, told in episodes.

How it uses TikTok

The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) to connect his own account. When he approves a finished video in the studio, Shadowville uploads it to his own TikTok account through the Content Posting API, either as a draft in his inbox or as a post he chose to publish. Nothing is posted without his approval, and Shadowville never posts to anyone else's account.

Shadowville is not a public service: there are no other users, no sign-ups and no ads.
