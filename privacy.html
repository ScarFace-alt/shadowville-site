Shadowville

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

What we collect

Shadowville is used only by its owner. When the owner connects TikTok, Shadowville receives from TikTok the owner's basic profile (open ID and display name) and access tokens, with the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

How it is used

Only to show which account is connected and to upload videos the owner has approved to that same account. Shadowville does not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are never sold, shared or sent to third parties other than TikTok's own API.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect at any time by revoking Shadowville in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and deleting the local token file. Revoked tokens stop working immediately.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.
